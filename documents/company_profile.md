
# TripMateAI Travel Company Profile

## Company Overview

TripMateAI is a fictional travel company based in Kerala, India.

The company focuses on providing organized holiday packages for customers who want to explore Kerala.

Its popular destinations include Munnar, Alleppey, and Wayanad.

The company aims to provide convenient travel information, transparent package pricing, and helpful customer support.

## Destinations

### Munnar

Munnar is a hill station known for tea plantations, misty mountains, waterfalls, and scenic viewpoints.

The TripMateAI Munnar Hills Escape package is designed for customers interested in nature and hill station experiences.

### Alleppey

Alleppey is famous for its backwaters, houseboats, canals, and peaceful village scenery.

The TripMateAI Alleppey Backwater Break package focuses on backwater experiences.

### Wayanad

Wayanad is known for forests, waterfalls, green landscapes, and nature attractions.

The TripMateAI Wayanad Nature Trail package is designed for customers interested in nature and outdoor experiences.

## Customer Support

TripMateAI's customer support assistant can:

- Explain the company's travel packages.
- Retrieve package information from the company database.
- Calculate package prices using stored rates.
- Explain company policies from approved documents.
- Look up synthetic demonstration booking records.

The AI assistant cannot confirm real-time travel availability, accept payments, or create real bookings.

## Official Demo Packages

The company currently has three demonstration packages:

- KV001: Munnar Hills Escape
- KV002: Alleppey Backwater Break
- KV003: Wayanad Nature Trail

For exact durations and prices, the assistant must retrieve information from the SQLite package database.

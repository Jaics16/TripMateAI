
# TripMateAI Customer Policies

## Booking Policy

TripMateAI is currently a demonstration travel support application.

The assistant can retrieve synthetic sample booking records from its database.

The assistant cannot create, modify, or confirm real customer bookings.

Customers must not interpret a demonstration booking record as a real reservation.

## Payment Policy

The demonstration application does not process payments.

The assistant must not request credit card numbers, bank account details, passwords, or payment credentials.

No payment transaction can be completed through this application.

## Cancellation Policy

TripMateAI's demonstration knowledge base does not contain an official cancellation or refund policy.

If a customer asks about cancellation charges, refund eligibility, or refund processing time, the assistant must explain that this information is not currently available in the approved company documents.

The assistant must not invent cancellation fees or promise refunds.

## Travel Availability Policy

The application does not connect to live hotel, transport, airline, or tour availability systems.

The assistant must not claim that a package is currently available for a particular travel date.

Package information in the database represents demonstration package data only.

## Package Pricing Policy

Package prices must be retrieved from the SQLite database.

The assistant must use the calculate_package_price tool when a customer asks for a total price.

Adult and child prices must not be guessed or invented.

The assistant must clearly identify the calculated total as a demonstration package estimate.

## Customer Privacy Policy

The assistant must not ask customers to share passwords, payment credentials, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Sample booking information is synthetic and intended only for software demonstration.

## Unsupported Questions

If the customer asks a question that cannot be answered using the approved company documents or database tools, the assistant must clearly say that the information is not available in the current TripMateAI knowledge base.

The assistant must not invent company policies, package features, prices, booking confirmations, or travel availability.
